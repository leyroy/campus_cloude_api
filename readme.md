# 🎓 CampusCloud – Modular School Management System

**CampusCloud** is a full-featured, multi-tenant School Management System tailored for Basic to Senior High Schools in Ghana and across Africa.

It empowers schools to efficiently manage academics, finance, communication, staff, students, and parents — all from one powerful, cloud-based platform.

---

## 🧩 Modules Overview

### 🔐 1. Authentication & Authorization
- Secure login for Admin, Teacher, Student, and Parent
- Multi-tenant data isolation
- Role-based access control
- Forgot/reset password
- OTP/Email verification (optional)

---

### 🏢 2. School (Tenant) Management
- Register and manage schools independently
- Multi-campus support (optional)
- School profile setup: logo, contact info, etc.
- Plan-based subscription (SaaS-ready)

---

### 👥 3. Staff Management
- Add/edit teachers and staff
- Assign to classes/subjects
- Staff attendance tracking
- Role and permission control

---

### 🎓 4. Student Management
- Add students manually or via CSV
- Assign to classes and parents
- Track profiles, progress, fees, and results
- Promote, transfer, or graduate

---

### 📚 5. Class & Subject Management
- Create and manage class structures
- Define subject lists per level
- Assign teachers to subjects
- Optional: Timetable support

---

### 📆 6. Attendance Tracking
- Daily student attendance by class
- Staff attendance logging
- Absentee and summary reports

---

### 📊 7. Results & Grading System
- Record class tests and exams
- Weighted grade calculations
- Generate student reports and transcripts
- Export to PDF

---

### 💰 8. Fees & Payment Management
- Define fee structures per program/class
- Record transactions and balances
- Generate receipts and track debtors
- Optional: Mobile Money / Paystack integration

---

### 📢 9. Communication Module
- In-app messaging between users
- Notice board system for announcements
- Optional SMS/email integration

---

### 📅 10. Academic Calendar & Events
- Set academic sessions and holidays
- Schedule key school events (PTA, exams, etc.)

---

### 🧾 11. Report Cards & Transcripts
- Auto-generate termly report cards (PDF)
- Include grading, comments, attendance
- Printable and downloadable formats

---

### 📈 12. Performance Analytics
- Visualize student progress and performance
- Monitor attendance and financial stats
- Teacher performance reports

---

### 🔐 13. Role & Permission Control
- Default roles: Admin, Teacher, Student, Parent
- Create custom roles
- Manage module access rights

---

### ⚙️ 14. Settings & Configuration
- School-wide settings (term dates, currency, grading scale)
- Custom fields for student/staff profiles

---

### 💳 15. SaaS Billing & Subscription *(Optional)*
- Admin dashboard for managing tenants
- Subscription plans: Free, Basic, Premium
- Invoice & billing history

---

### 📂 16. Document Uploads
- Upload and manage documents per user
- Supports images, PDFs, and other file types

---

### 📖 17. Library Management *(Optional)*
- Manage book inventory
- Issue and return tracking

---

### 🍽️ 18. Canteen & Inventory *(Optional)*
- Meal plans for students/staff
- Track inventory and orders
